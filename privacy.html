Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This policy explains how Playable Pet Games handles information submitted for personalized browser mini-game orders.

Information we receive

  • Your email address and messages.
  • Pet photos, pet names and customization instructions you choose to send.
  • Payment confirmation information necessary to match a PayPal transaction to an order.

How we use it

We use submitted information to communicate with you, create the requested game, process revisions, deliver the private game link and provide customer support.

Payments

Payments are completed on PayPal-hosted pages. This website does not collect or store card numbers, bank account details or PayPal passwords. PayPal processes payment and identifying information under its own privacy terms.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell customer information to advertisers. Files may be retained for the time reasonably needed to complete the service, support revisions, maintain transaction records and comply with legal obligations.

Deletion requests

You may request deletion of a submitted pet photo by emailing ereyaim@gmail.com. Some transaction records may need to be retained where required by law or payment-provider rules.

Do not send payment passwords, full card details or sensitive identity documents by email.

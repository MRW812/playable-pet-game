FAQ

Questions before ordering

What do I receive?

A personalized browser mini game, private shareable link and the revisions included in the selected package.

What pet photo works best?

A clear front-facing or slightly angled photo with good lighting. Avoid heavy filters and photos where the pet is very small.

When does production begin?

Production begins after payment is confirmed and all required pet photos and customization details are received.

What happens after I pay?

Email the pet photo and customization details to ereyaim@gmail.com. Include the PayPal transaction ID so the order can be matched quickly.

Do I need to install an app?

No. The game opens directly in a modern phone or desktop browser.

Can I request changes?

Yes. Revision rounds depend on the selected package and cover the agreed visual and text details.

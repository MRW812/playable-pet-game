Refund Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Before production begins

You may request a full refund before production begins.

After production begins

Because each game is custom-made, refund decisions after production begins take into account the work already completed, the selected package and applicable consumer law.

Non-delivery or material defect

If the service is not delivered or the delivered game has a material defect that cannot reasonably be corrected, we will correct the issue or provide an appropriate refund.

How to request help

Email ereyaim@gmail.com with the order email, PayPal transaction ID and a description of the issue. Refunds, where approved, are returned through the original payment method when possible.

This policy does not limit rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable consumer law.
